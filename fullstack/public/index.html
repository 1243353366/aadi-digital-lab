01 — About the Lab

A place where curiosity becomes capability.

The Lab is a personal research environment for studying systems — technical, social, and informational. Every investigation starts with a question and ends with something you can check: a method, a dataset, a tool, or a well-sourced writeup.

First principles

Problems get broken down to what is verifiably true, then understanding is rebuilt from there — no inherited assumptions, no vibes-based analysis.

Structured analysis

Every claim carries a confidence level, and every conclusion traces back to named sources. Techniques borrow from structured analytic tradecraft.

Build in public

Methods, notebooks, dead ends, and failures get documented anyway — that is what makes the results reproducible and the process honest.

02 — Research & OSINT

Open sources, closed loops.

OSINT here means reproducible open-source research: an explicit collection plan, structured source validation, and version-controlled notebooks. Provenance first, conclusions second.

Ethics & responsible use

Everything published in the Lab is built from publicly available, legally obtained sources. These methods exist for research, journalism, and public-interest work — never for harassment, doxxing, or unlawful activity. Verify before you amplify.

03 — AI Tools & Experiments

Models as instruments, not oracles.

AI in the Lab is treated like lab equipment: measured, benchmarked, and never trusted on faith. These are the experiments currently on the bench.

LIVE

Retrieval pipelines for research corpora

Chunking and embedding search over thousands of pages of collected documents, so a finding is always one query away from its source.

EXPERIMENT

Agentic research workflows

Small multi-step agents that draft collection plans and pre-triage sources — every output is reviewed by a human before it counts.

PROTOTYPE

Synthetic data for testing

Generating realistic-but-fake datasets to stress-test detection rules and pipelines without touching real personal data.

NOTES

LLM-assisted note triage

Turning raw, messy research notes into structured, linked analysis that survives contact with the next investigation.
